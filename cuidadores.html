When design makes the next step

Social impact · Informal caregiving

Website redesign · Copywriting · UX Strategy

Tools

Wix · Tally · Copywriting

Timeline

6 weeks

Situation

Do you look after someone?

Cuidadores is a Porto-based nonprofit founded in 2016 that supports informal carers before, during, and after their caregiving experience. Most carers work invisibly: at home, around the clock, caring for someone with dementia, mental illness, or dependency.

Many don't call themselves caregivers at all. They're too absorbed in the other person to look for support - and too used to not being the one who needs it.

The existing site described services. But it didn't invite carers to feel that this space was built for them. It informed. It didn't welcome.

Before

A site that spoke about the institution,
not to the person at the door.

The original site was structured around the organisation's logic - services, events, reports. Institutional in tone, complex in navigation, heavy with information that required a reading session, not a moment of relief.

Someone arriving worn down needed to find answers to their needs, not wade through content written for the organisation's benefit.

Before

After

What was needed

Three audiences.
Three very different conversations.

One coherent presence where each person immediately felt they were in the right place.

  • Carers: to feel recognised before being asked for anything
  • Volunteers: to understand exactly what they're committing to
  • Partners: strategic language and documented impact metrics

The approach

Before opening the editor,
a 23-question diagnostic.

Designed as a first experience of the new identity: calm photography, generous spacing, gentle language. Not a form - a conversation that set the tone for everything that followed.

The diagnostic form - itself a first experience of the new identity.

Every page was rebuilt around one question: what does this person need to feel before they act? New CTAs were written for each audience - not commands, but invitations.

"Aceitar apoio" · "Começar aqui" · "Juntar-me à equipa"

What was delivered

Six pages.
Three conversations. One identity.

  • 6 pages + visual identity system (Nunito + DM Sans · teal · terracotta · cream)
  • Copywriting for 3 user profiles - tone, language, CTAs adapted to each
  • ESG partnerships page - repositioned with strategic language, SDG alignment, and impact metrics
  • Configured forms for each audience with custom fields and placeholders
  • Team page with personal commitment statements
  • Mobile-optimised · Complete legal footer
