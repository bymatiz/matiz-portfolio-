About

The distance between what a brand is and what it shows online.

I'm Sandra Guiomar, and that gap is where I work. Under the Matiz brand, I help producers, studios, and mission-driven organisations translate rich, embodied identities into digital presences that hold up — without creating dependency on developers for every update.

My background sits at the intersection of digital strategy and brand narrative. I pay close attention to what a brand communicates before anyone reads a word: the texture of a page, whether it breathes, what the eye does first.

Currently focused on Portugal's agri-food interior — working with producers whose craft deserves the kind of digital presence that matches what ends up in the jar, the bottle, the basket.

How I work

The craft behind the strategy.

Identity before interface

A digital presence that doesn't reflect who you actually are will always feel wrong — to you and to your audience. I start with brand identity, not with platform choice. The platform follows.

Autonomy as a design principle

Every site I build, you should be able to run. No recurring agency dependency for a banner update or a new product page. The build is complete when you don't need me anymore.

Craft and digital in dialogue

The producers and studios I work with have invested years in their craft. The digital work earns its place by being legible to that investment — not flattening it into a template.

Roots in the territory

I work extensively with Portugal's agri-food interior — producers whose geography, tradition, and production methods are part of the product's value. Making that legible digitally is both a creative and a strategic challenge.

Let's talk

A conversation before a proposal.

I work with 3–5 clients at a time. If you're building something with a distinct identity and want a digital presence to match, get in touch. I'll tell you honestly whether we're a fit.
