Situation

Knowledge held in hands,
not screens.

Pátina was born from a belief that certain kinds of knowledge - the kind that lives in the hands, accumulated through years of practice with clay, glass, wood, iron - deserves a contemporary home without losing what makes it irreplaceable.

The vision: a multi-generational studio where artists and artisans share traditional skills reinterpreted through contemporary sensibilities. A place of deliberate making. Where the material pushes back.

Developed as a practice project during a no-code programme - now in active transition to a real business.

The universe

Heritage dressed
in contemporaneity.

Form · Olaria

Thread · Tecelagem

Grain · Marcenaria

The visual world of Pátina needed to carry the sensory richness of working with materials - the weight of wet clay, the resistance of wood grain, the pull of thread on a loom - without becoming nostalgic or inaccessible.

What was needed

More than a website.
A system.

A creative studio with a varied programme needs infrastructure that works quietly in the background - so the people running it can focus on the work, not the administration.

  • Present the offer beautifully, in a way that earns the craft's credibility
  • Let participants filter by technique, duration, and level
  • Handle registrations without friction - for participants and organisers
  • Manage the programme autonomously, without depending on IT

"No-code isn't a limitation - it's a different kind of architecture. The constraint forces clearer thinking about what the system actually needs to do."

The approach

Three layers.
One coherent experience.

The platform was built in three connected layers, each with a distinct role - and all invisible to the participant, who experiences only a fluid, considered journey from discovery to registration.

Layer 01 · Wix

Landing page

Editorial photography of clay, linen, and wood. Copywriting that invites the sensory experience of working with materials before asking for any commitment.

Layer 02 · Airtable

Workshop database

21 programmes · 6 fields · 4 views including gallery. Relational structure between techniques and programmes - the backbone the participant never sees.

Layer 03 · Softr

Dynamic web app

Connected to Airtable in real time: search and filters · programme detail · registration form · complete authentication (Sign Up · Log In · Account Settings).

The atelier as a living space - the platform was designed to carry that feeling, not flatten it.

What was delivered

From landing page
to full platform.

  • Landing page with visual identity and sensory copywriting
  • Airtable database - 21 programmes · 6 fields · 4 views
  • Softr web app with dynamic lists, search filters, and registration form

  • Built without a single line of code
